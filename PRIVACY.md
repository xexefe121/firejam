# FireJam privacy policy

*Last updated: 28 October 2026*

FireJam lets friends and family watch TV together from different places. This policy explains what the FireJam app and the FireJam server collect, why, and what you can do about it. FireJam is open source, so you can check everything here in the code at https://github.com/xexefe121/firejam.

Questions or requests: **unityweb9@gmail.com**

## What FireJam collects

- **Your profile.** The name you choose, a profile colour, and a city. The city is picked from your TV's time zone, so FireJam can show friends your local time. FireJam does not use GPS or look up your location from your IP address.
- **Friends.** Who you've added, and friend requests you've sent or received.
- **Watching.** The jams you start or join, what's playing in them, and a short history of what you watched, when, and with whom.
- **Chat and reactions.** Messages and reactions are relayed live to the people in your jam. Offensive words are hidden before anyone sees them. The server keeps only the last few messages of each jam in memory, so a message can be attached to a report; it does not store chat otherwise.
- **Reports and blocks.** If you report someone, FireJam stores the report (who reported whom, the reason, when, and the reported message if there was one) so it can be reviewed. The people you block are stored with your profile.
- **What's playing in other apps (optional).** On Android TV, and on Fire TV when access is granted, FireJam can read the title and play or pause state of what's playing in other apps (for example Prime Video) to keep a Prime Video jam in sync and to show friends what you're watching. You can turn sharing off in FireJam, or remove the access in your TV's settings.
- **Sign-in identifiers.** A random id and secret token for your profile, a random id for your TV, and a one-way hash of your TV's active profile, so the TV can sign you in without a password. The hash cannot be turned back into your Fire TV profile.

FireJam has no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking across other apps or websites.

## Who can see it

- **Your friends** see your name, city, local time, what you're watching now and what you watched recently. Ghost mode hides you from the Jam Map, and you can turn off activity sharing.
- **Everyone** can see public jams on the Jam Map, including the names of the people in them. Friends-only and invite-only jams are not shown to strangers.
- **People in your jam** see your chat messages, reactions and playback actions (for example "Ela paused").

## Community rules and moderation

Be kind. FireJam hides offensive words in names and chat. You can block anyone, which hides their messages and reactions from you and stops their requests and invites, and you can report a person or a message from the TV. A jam's host can remove someone from the jam. Reports are reviewed, and profiles that break these rules can be suspended. A profile that several different people report is suspended automatically until it has been reviewed.

## Services FireJam uses

- **Amazon Web Services.** The FireJam server runs on AWS in Sydney, Australia, and stores profiles there.
- **Amazon Bedrock.** When someone in a jam chooses "Pick for us", the first names, cities, local times and recent titles of the people in that jam are sent to Amazon Bedrock (Amazon Nova) to suggest a film. Requests may be processed in AWS regions in the Asia Pacific. AWS does not use them to train models.

FireJam does not sell or rent your information, and shares it with no one else unless the law requires it.

## Keeping and deleting your information

Your profile stays until you ask us to delete it. Email unityweb9@gmail.com from the address you'd like us to reply to, with your FireJam name, and we will delete your profile, friends and history within 30 days. Uninstalling FireJam removes everything it stored on your TV.

## Children

FireJam is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created a profile, email us and we will delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
